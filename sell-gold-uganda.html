Sellers, Miners & Brokers

Where to sell gold in Kampala.

If you're searching for where to sell gold in Kampala, or looking for gold sellers who'll actually test your material before naming a price, KampGold UG Ltd's Kampala office on Next Minute Street is a walk-in option — no appointment needed for individual sellers with jewellery or a small quantity of raw gold.

What you can sell here

  • Raw gold nuggets straight from a mining site
  • Panned and alluvial gold sourced from rivers
  • Gold dust
  • Scrap and broken gold jewellery
  • Smelted bars

How the sale works

Every sale opens with an independent weigh-in and purity test — fire assay for raw material, XRF for jewellery and finished pieces — done in front of you. Only once that's confirmed do we discuss a price, based on tested purity and the day's market rate. If you accept, settlement is typically the same day, in cash or by bank transfer.

Selling as a miner or broker

If you have regular volume to move — as an artisanal miner or a licensed broker — we can set up a standing arrangement so each delivery doesn't need renegotiating from scratch, with scheduled collection available in Mubende, Kassanda, Busia, Karamoja, Mbarara, and Jinja.
