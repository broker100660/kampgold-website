Two tests, and you watch both.

We use the two standard methods in the gold trade for confirming purity. The same testing applies to gold we buy from you and gold we sell to you.

Fire assay

A definitive read on raw material. We use it when the exact purity of raw gold, nuggets or dust decides the price.

Best for raw gold

XRF analysis

X-ray fluorescence gives a fast, non-destructive check that suits jewellery and finished pieces.

Best for jewellery and finished pieces

Testing happens in front of you, not behind closed doors. Results are explained in plain terms before pricing is discussed.

Questions people ask first.

Do I need an appointment to sell gold?

Individual sellers can walk in to our Kampala office without one. Miners and brokers with regular volume can arrange scheduled collection.

How is the price decided?

From the tested purity, the weight and the day’s market rate. We weigh and test in front of you before any price is named.

How and when do I get paid?

By cash or bank transfer, typically settled the same day terms are agreed.

What gold do you sell?

Tested gold bars, nuggets and gold dust, to buyers, jewellers and investors. Contact us for current availability and a quote.

Can you hold gold for me?

Yes. We offer short-term monitored storage for clients awaiting a sale, export clearance or collection. It’s arranged case by case, so tell us the volume and how long you need it.

Do you handle exports?

We give guidance and paperwork support, but the export itself goes through Uganda’s licensed export channels. We help you understand what documentation is needed and where it comes from.
