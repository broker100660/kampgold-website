What We Do

Trading services built around proof, not paperwork.

Whether you're a miner with raw gold, a broker moving volume, or an individual with jewellery to sell — each service below runs through the same tested, transparent process.

01 · Buying

Gold Buying

We purchase raw gold, nuggets, panned and alluvial gold, and gold dust from walk-in sellers and scheduled deliveries. Every purchase starts with a weigh-in on a calibrated scale, followed by a purity test — nothing is priced from a visual estimate.

  • Walk-in purchases at our Kampala office, no appointment required for individual sellers
  • Scheduled collection available for miners and brokers with regular volume
  • Weight and purity confirmed in front of the seller before a price is named
02 · Testing

Purity Testing & Assaying

We test using fire assay and XRF (X-ray fluorescence) analysis, the two standard methods used across the gold trade for confirming karat and purity. Fire assay is used for a definitive read on raw material; XRF gives a fast, non-destructive check that's well suited to jewellery and finished pieces.

  • Testing is carried out in front of the client, not behind closed doors
  • Results are explained in plain terms before pricing is discussed
  • Applies to raw gold, scrap, and jewellery alike
03 · Storage

Secure Storage

For clients awaiting a sale, export clearance, or collection, we offer short-term monitored storage rather than requiring immediate pickup or sale on the same day.

  • Suited to consignments in transit or awaiting documentation
  • Arranged case-by-case — get in touch to discuss volume and duration
04 · Partnerships

Broker & Mining Partnerships

For brokers and small-scale miners with regular production, we set up standing purchase arrangements — an agreed process for testing, pricing, and settlement so each delivery doesn't need to be renegotiated from scratch.

  • Consistent testing and pricing terms across repeat transactions
  • Reach extends to Mubende, Kassanda, Busia, Karamoja, Mbarara, and Jinja by arrangement
05 · Export

Export Documentation Support

Clients moving gold through Uganda's licensed export channels can get guidance and paperwork support from us — we don't replace the licensed export process, but we help make sure sellers understand what documentation is needed and where it comes from.

06 · Advisory

Market Advisory

Before you sell anywhere — to us or elsewhere — we'll give you a straight read on where gold prices currently sit and what your specific material is likely worth once tested. No pressure to sell on the spot.

How It Works

Four steps, same order, every time.

01

Bring your gold in

Walk in with raw gold, jewellery, or a consignment — or arrange a scheduled collection for larger volumes.

02

We weigh and test it

Independent weigh-in and purity testing (fire assay or XRF), witnessed by you before any number is discussed.

03

You get a transparent offer

A price based on tested purity and the day's market rate — no guesswork, no pressure to accept on the spot.

04

You get paid promptly

Cash or bank transfer, typically settled the same day terms are agreed.
