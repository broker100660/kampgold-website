Questions

Before you walk in.

How is my gold's purity determined?

We use fire assay and XRF testing on-site, done in front of you. You'll see the weight and purity result before any price is discussed.

How fast do I get paid?

Most transactions are settled the same day, either in cash or by bank transfer — typically within 24 hours of agreeing a price.

Do you buy jewellery and scrap gold?

Yes. Jewellery, broken pieces, and scrap are tested the same way as raw gold — purity first, then a price based on actual gold content.

Can you collect gold from outside Kampala?

For brokers and miners with regular volume, we can arrange scheduled collection in several regions — get in touch to set this up.

Is KampGold licensed to trade gold?

Yes — KampGold UG Ltd is a registered company with a valid TIN, operating within Uganda's licensed trading framework.
